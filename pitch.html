Decentralized Peer-Reviewed Framework

Onucrom operates on a structured, human-in-the-loop peer due-diligence framework. We are intentionally bypassing complex blockchain overhead during this initial rollout to maintain simplicity and speed. However, as our scale grows and data requirements expand, we remain open to revisiting distributed ledger architectures in the future.

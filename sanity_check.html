Milestone 1 of 4: Phase 1
Phase 1: Entity & Channel Verification
1. What is the name of the investment/organization?
2. Where did you first discover this investment opportunity?
3. What is the legal formation of the enterprise?
4. Is there a verified physical office or operational site you can visit?
5. Is the company registered with RJSC, Bangladesh Bank, or BSEC?
Phase 2: Financial Terms & Return Logic
6. What is the stated annual return percentage (%)?
7. How are the returns described?
8. What is the investment instrument type?
9. When do payouts begin relative to capital investment?
10. Why is the business seeking retail capital instead of a bank loan (9–14% rate)?
Phase 3: Legal Protections & Pressure Tactics
11. What documentation will you sign BEFORE sending funds?
12. Is there sales pressure or "limited quota" urgency applied?
13. Does the offering team provide historical audited financial statements?
14. Is capital pooled into a personal bank account or a business corporate account?
15. Are you offered commissions to recruit other investors?
16. What is the exit / capital lock-in period?
Phase 4: Operational Due Diligence
17. Have you met the founders/key managers in person?
18. Are the underlying assets (e.g., cows, land, inventory) insured?
19. Does the business rely on a single client, supplier, or key person?
20. Is there an independent party or trustee monitoring funds?
21. Submit Role (Help build transparency)

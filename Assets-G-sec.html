📚 Investor Education: Treasury Securities

T-Bills vs. T-Bonds

Treasury Bills (T-Bills): Short-term investments (91, 182, 364 days). Issued at a discount and redeemed at par value.

Treasury Bonds (BGTB): Long-term instruments (2 to 20 years) paying fixed semi-annual coupon interest.

How to Purchase in Bangladesh

  1. Open a Business Partner ID (BP-ID) with Bangladesh Bank via a scheduled bank or Primary Dealer.
  2. Open a Market Infrastructure (MI) Module trading account.
  3. Submit bids during scheduled primary auctions or trade on the DSE secondary market.

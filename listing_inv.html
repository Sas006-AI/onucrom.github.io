Enlist Your Project

For project sponsors and investors raising capital in Bangladesh. Takes about 5 minutes.

Before you start

  • Free and human-run. A person at Onucrom reviews every entry. Review sittings are held daily at 12:00 and 18:00 (Dhaka time), and human verification can take a few hours. You get an email with the decision either way.
  • Limited on purpose. We are a small team and accept no more than 30 new entries a day. One person (same email or same mobile number) may hold up to 2 active listings.
  • Who may enlist. Only a project sponsor or an investor authorised to enlist the project.
  • Private vs public. Your name, email, mobile number and social profiles are used only for verification and are never shown. Fields marked PUBLIC appear on the site only if we approve the listing for display.
  • Links: public and safe only. Give https:// links that already work for anyone. We do not accept programs, scripts, archives, download links or shortened links. Every link you share becomes public with your listing, and by submitting you consent to that. Onucrom does not control third-party sites and accepts no liability for them. Self-hosted proof is allowed as a view-only Google Drive / Google Docs link or a GitHub page link.
  • No guarantees. Claims of guaranteed, assured or risk-free returns are not accepted. False or misleading entries are disqualified, and we keep a record of declined entries.
  • 3-month validity. Approvals last 3 months. We remind you before expiry; renew or update with your Reference ID. Read the full listing terms.

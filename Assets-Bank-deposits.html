📚 Investor Education: Bank Deposits

Deposit Insurance Scheme

In Bangladesh, bank deposits are insured up to BDT 1,00,000 (currently being reviewed for an increase) per depositor per bank by the Deposit Insurance Trust Fund (DITF) managed by Bangladesh Bank.

FDR vs DPS

Fixed Deposit Receipt (FDR): A lump sum deposited for a fixed maturity period.

Deposit Pension Scheme (DPS): A recurring deposit where small monthly installments build up over a long tenure.

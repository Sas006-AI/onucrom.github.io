Macro
1 Bangladesh Bank plans to introduce a fully floating exchange rate system within a month, allowing buyers and sellers to negotiate foreign currency prices directly, BB Governor has said at a consultation meeting.

3 Bangladesh risks remaining trapped in a low-growth cycle, with average economic growth projected at 4.2% over the decade to 2035 unless it undertakes reforms, the World Bank has warned. Sustained reforms, however, could lift average annual growth to 5.7%.
4 Manufacturing employment fell from 9.5 Mn to 8.1 lakh despite sustained growth in industrial output, highlighting a widening disconnect between economic expansion and employment generation, the study found.
5 Bangladesh is set to receive up to EUR 402 Mn in development assistance from Germany under a latest development-cooperation programme as the two countries have agreed to expand cooperation in renewable energy, energy efficiency, skills development, and sustainable infrastructure.
6 Net sales of national savings certificates increased in Q1FY27 as concerns over the safety of bank deposits and lower deposit rates encouraged households to shift their savings to government-backed investment schemes.
Sector & Industries
1 Loan defaulters who have secured stay orders from courts have been declared eligible to receive loans under Bangladesh Bank’s BDT 600 Bn incentive schemes.
2 BB plans to cap the amount a single borrower can take in loans, as well as the share of corporate lending in a bank’s portfolio. Under the Bank Company Act, a single borrower can take a maximum of 25% of a bank’s paid-up capital. However, there is no limit on how much a borrower can take from the banking sector as a whole.
3 BB has instructed Standard Chartered Bangladesh (SCB) to retain 5% of annual profits under the Workers' Profit Participation Fund (WPPF) as retained earnings until legal complications over the payment are resolved.
4 Lending through agent banking channels expanded by more than 38% year-on-year to BDT 441.1 Bn at the end of September 2026. The average loan size is BDT 50,000, and it mostly goes to shops and micro-entrepreneurs.
5 The country’s economy could lose BDT 20–22 Bn each day if ready-made garment production stays shut amid the ongoing labour unrest, the Bangladesh Garment Buying House Association warned on Thursday.
6 Minimum wage wrangles for the garment sector get complicated following sharp pushback from both factory owners and labour bodies against the Centre for Policy Dialogue (CPD)-broached pay recommendations.
7 Bangladesh now has 302 LEED-certified green factories, the highest of any country, as 12 more apparel, textile and accessories factories recently earned certification from the US Green Building Council.
8 Imports from Bangladesh fell 5.48% to EUR 1.01 Bn from that of EUR 1.07 Bn in the same period past year.
9 The government will build a 70 MW solar power plant in Matarbari at cost of BDT 6.05 Bn, with the European Investment Bank<https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/public/last-page/tk-605b-matarbari-solar-project-faces-cost-scrutiny-1795199351> (EIB) expected to provide the lion's share of the cost.
Stocks
1 T+1 trade settlement cycle to begin in Jan: The Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE) has decided to begin the T+1 trade settlement cycle on January 4 next year, with the expectation that a shortened cycle would boost trade volume.
2 Order management: Dhaka bourse seeks local vendor to cut costs: The Dhaka bourse plans to appoint a local vendor instead of a foreign one to provide Order Management System (OMS) services to brokerage firms in order to cut costs. DSE chairman has said the DSE was also thinking of establishing a FinTech company in an effort to ensure the services.
3 DSE sees BDT 1.71 Bn foreign turnover in early November: Foreign turnover, combining the buying and selling of shares at the Dhaka bourse, was reported at BDT 1.71 Bn in the first half of November, according to a fortnightly report by the Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE).
4 Derivatives debut historic, but success takes time: BSEC chief: Bangladesh's move to introduce derivatives trading in the capital market marks a historic milestone, but significant trading activity is unlikely to emerge immediately, said BSEC Chairman.
5 Auditors flag survival risks at 60 listed firms, sparking investor concerns: The number of listed companies facing doubts over their ability to continue operations has risen sharply, with auditors flagging 60 firms as going concerns in their latest financial statements, up from 37 in April, according to the Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE).
6 BSEC eases foreign dividend payout rules for listed firms: The Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC) has relaxed dividend disbursement rules for listed companies, exempting legitimate delays in payments to foreign shareholders from penalties when the delays arise from procedural requirements for obtaining tax clearance.
7 Mismatches found in financial reporting by 4 firms: Auditors found accounting discrepancies, governance weaknesses and issues tied to regulatory compliance in the FY26 financial statements of four listed companies, raising questions about the reliability of their financial reporting and internal controls. The companies are Techno Drugs, Aman Cotton Fibrous, Safko Spinning Mills and Legacy Footwear.

Macro The International Monetary Fund (IMF) is likely to seek time-bound measures to resolve problems in vulnerable banks and set specific targets and deadlines for domestic revenue collection as conditions for a new loan programme, according to government officials.
Macro Bangladeshi companies get about 75% of their financing from banks, leaving corporate growth heavily dependent on lenders while equity and bonds remain underused as sources of long-term capital.

Macro Chattogram Custom House's revenue collection rose nearly 11% year-on-year in Q1FY27, but still fell BDT 99.49 Bn short of its revenue target. The shortfall came as total import volume through the customs house declined 4.1% year-on-year in September, while the assessable value of imports fell 2.98%.
Macro Dubai-based multinational DP World will handle nearly half of Chittagong seaport's seaborne trade containers under a deal signed with Bangladesh on Thursday to operate the New-mooring Container Terminal.
Macro The National Special Economic Zone (NSEZ) in Mirsarai remains largely vacant, hindered by infrastructure delays, utility shortages, and a complete absence of supporting worker ecosystems.
Sector & Industries Bangladesh Bank has sought BDT 600 Bn annually from the government<https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/first-page/bb-hands-govt-tk-600b-annual-bill-for-banking-sector-recovery-1791654042> over the next five to eight years for recovery of the banking industry that witnessed a massive looting, said the central bank chief.
Sector & Industries The Bangladesh Bank has broadened the scope of Non-Resident Convertible Taka Accounts (NRCTAs), allowing authorised dealers (ADs), in addition to offshore banking units (OBUs), to open and maintain the accounts for non-resident Bangladeshis (NRBs).
Sector & Industries Commercial banks traded more than USD 200 Mn in the interbank market on Thursday, marking a very high level of dollar transactions in recent times. A senior Bangladesh Bank official said that there is currently a relatively high supply of dollars, while demand in the market is comparatively low.
Sector & Industries Bangladesh Bank (BB) has instructed banks and financial institutions to expedite the disbursement of the BDT 50 Bn refinancing funds for cottage, micro, small, and medium enterprises (CMSMEs) and meet their full distribution targets by November.
Sector & Industries Holders of a high-yield two-year Treasury bond showed limited interest in selling it back to the government, offering only about 30% of the bond for sale. Bangladesh Bank did not disclose the cut-off price, leaving the discount or premium paid to sellers unclear.
Sector & Industries The Local Government Division (LGD) has asked city corporations, municipalities and district administrations to take steps to make transactions through Bangla QR mandatory for retail business establishments and merchants as a condition for issuing and renewing licences.

Annual domestic steel consumption has fallen to ~4.0 Mn tonnes from ~7.0 Mn tonnes previously. The government’s share of steel consumption has also dropped from around 60% to 20%.
Sector & Industries Bangladesh's RMG exports grew 6.10% year-on-year in Q1FY27, reaching USD 10,578.56 Mn against USD 9,970.42 Mn in Q1FY26.
Sector & Industries Bangladesh's RMG exports to non-traditional markets grew by 9.40% year-on-year to USD 1.81 Bn in Q1FY27, outpacing the 6.10% growth in overall apparel exports.
Sector & Industries Petrobangla has requested an urgent release of a minimum subsidy of BDT 32 Bn from the Finance Division to settle unpaid LNG import bills for September 2026.
Sector & Industries QatarEnergy has agreed to supply one LNG cargo under its initial long-term supply contract at USD 13 /MMBTu, less than half the spot LNG price in a volatile market.
Sector & Industries Bangladesh Telecommunication Regulatory Commission (BTRC) is considering a proposal to raise the maximum Emergency Balance limit for mobile subscribers from BDT 200 to BDT 500, but has put the proposal on hold for now to scrutinise the conditions attached to the service and ensure that customers are not adversely affected.
Sector & Industries Fair Electronics Ltd has resumed production of Samsung-brand refrigerators in Bangladesh after a hiatus of nearly two years, restarting manufacturing at its factory in Shibpur, Narsingdi. Fair Electronics has an annual refrigerator production capacity of around 0.7 Mn units.

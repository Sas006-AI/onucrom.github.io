The Virtual Assessor ? Critique Questions

The user clicks [Run Assessment]. The system doesn't just give a score ? it generates specific critique observations.

Critique Logic Matrix

User Input

Critique Generated

Promised return > 15% AND guaranteed

"A guaranteed 15%+ return in today's Bangladesh market is exceptional. Banks offer 6-8% FDR. The stock market averages 12-14% with risk. Anything offering significantly more with a guarantee should be treated with extreme skepticism. Ask: what is the underlying asset generating this return, and why isn't institutional money chasing it?"

Promised return > 24%

"Returns above 24% are in the territory of the top 1% of businesses globally. In Bangladesh, even successful SMEs rarely sustain this. If the business truly generates this, why would they share it with small investors instead of scaling with institutional capital? The math demands an extraordinary explanation."

Firm structure = "Unclear" OR "Individual"

"Investing in an unclear entity or an individual rather than a registered company removes almost all legal protection. If something goes wrong, you have no RJSC record, no regulatory recourse, and no way to establish ownership. This is the single biggest structural risk in private investing."

Investment type = "Deposit Scheme" AND "Unclear entity"

"Unregistered deposit-taking is illegal in Bangladesh under the Deposit Protection Act and Bangladesh Bank regulations. Only scheduled banks and licensed NBFIs can accept deposits from the public. If this is being pitched as a 'deposit scheme' by an unlicensed entity, it is likely operating outside the law."

Source = "WhatsApp / Social Media forward"

"Investment opportunities that circulate primarily via forwarded messages rather than formal channels have a higher incidence of fraud. Legitimate businesses seeking investment typically have a verifiable presence beyond social media. Ask: can you find their office, their registration, their tax filings?"

Return starts "Immediately / Within 30 days"

"Immediate returns from a business investment are structurally unusual. Real businesses take time to deploy capital and generate revenue. Promises of quick payouts are characteristic of schemes that use new investor money to pay earlier investors ? the classic hallmark of a Ponzi structure."

Documentation = "None"

"No legitimate investment should proceed without documentation. At minimum: trade license, RJSC incorporation certificate (for companies), audited or reviewed financial statements, and a formal investment agreement. If none of these exist, the investment exists only in conversation ? and conversation doesn't hold up in court."

Pressure = "Yes, urgent"

"Urgency is a manipulation tactic, not an investment feature. Real investment opportunities are evaluated over days or weeks, with legal review, site visits, and reference checks. Anyone pushing you to decide quickly is trying to prevent exactly the kind of scrutiny this tool provides."

Question 8 (bank loan logic) = "No good explanation"

"This is the question that unravels most bad investments. If a business can genuinely generate returns high enough to pay you 18-24%, they could borrow from a bank at 9-12% and keep the difference. The only reasons to seek expensive retail capital are: (a) banks have rejected them after due diligence, or (b) they don't intend to repay. Either way, you should understand why before proceeding."

Return type = "Guaranteed" AND Investment type = "Equity"

"There is a fundamental contradiction here. Equity means you own part of the business and share in its profits and losses. 'Guaranteed return' means you are promised a fixed outcome regardless of business performance. You cannot have both. If someone is offering 'equity with guaranteed returns,' they either don't understand what they're selling, or they're structuring something that is legally debt but calling it equity ? usually to avoid regulatory scrutiny."

Sector = "Trading" AND Description is vague

"'Trading' and 'import-export' are the most commonly abused categories in investment fraud because they are difficult to verify. Goods move, prices fluctuate, inventory is hard to audit. If the business cannot show: specific products, specific suppliers, specific buyers, warehousing, and shipping documentation ? treat the vagueness as a warning."

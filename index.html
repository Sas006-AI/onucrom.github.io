Macro
1 Open-handed tax incentives and other business-boosting budgetary measures have yet to fully satisfy multinational companies (MNCs) operating in Bangladesh in an uninspiring signal for fresh foreign investment in the country. Several key initiatives and reforms to attract foreign direct investment are on in Bangladesh though central-bank data show net inflows faced a 28% year-on-year drop during H12026.
2 A delegation of Japanese investors has announced plans to invest an initial USD 1.2 Bn in Bangladesh's tourism and hospitality sector, opening up fresh prospects for foreign investment in the largely untapped tourism industry.
3 Artificial intelligence is likely to negatively impact the country’s aggregate labour demand by 2.0% as its working-age population grows, according to an International Monetary Fund analysis.
4 The industries ministry shared the information through a letter on October 7 to the central bank, and the banking regulator today asked all authorised dealer banks to take necessary action accordingly.
5 Bangladesh Bank's BDT 200 Bn refinancing scheme to revive closed factories has gained some momentum, with loan disbursements rising to BDT 5.60 Bn by 18 October, although most applications remain under processing.
6 Stagnation in the country’s import trade has begun to ease with positive growth in LC openings. During 2MFY27, LC openings increased by nearly 18% year-over-year. Additionally, LC settlements witnessed growth of over 10%.
7 Bangladesh could lose up to BDT 17 Bn in annual revenue if it eliminates import duties on goods from the six-member Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) under a proposed free-trade agreement (FTA), prompting calls for a phased tariff reduction to limit the fiscal impact.
8 Vietnam and four European nations outside the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland, signed a free trade agreement in Hanoi on Sunday, seeking to diversify their markets as US tariffs disrupt global commerce.
Sector & Industries
1 Treasury bill interest rates rose sharply on Sunday as the government’s growing borrowing demand coincided with tighter bank liquidity after state-owned Sonali Bank lent BDT 70 Bn to financially troubled National Bank, turning itself from a major lender into a borrower.
2 Several banks that initially expressed interest in acquiring Standard Chartered Bangladesh's retail banking business have stepped back, citing concerns over the cost and complexity of absorbing around 1,500 employees, differences in deposit pricing, and customer behaviour.
3 The Bangladesh Telecommunication Regulatory Commission (BTRC) has decided to conduct a study to identify remote, hard-to-reach, underserved and disaster-prone areas where telecom services are inadequate or of poor quality and explore ways to ensure reliable, high-quality connectivity.
4 Bangladesh's largest LNG supplier, QatarEnergy, has delivered far fewer cargoes than committed under its long-term supply agreement, forcing the country to turn to the volatile spot market and alternative suppliers at significantly higher prices.
Stocks
1 BSEC proposes 50% IPO debt repayment cap, mandatory escrow accounts: The Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC) has proposed raising the cap on using initial public offering (IPO) and rights offer (RPO) proceeds to repay existing loans from 30% to 50%. It would also make escrow accounts mandatory to ensure the money goes to its declared purposes.
2 BRAC Bank, IFPRI to build digital financing ecosystem for farmers: BRAC Bank and the International Food Policy Research Institute (IFPRI) have partnered to expand digital payments and access to formal credit across Bangladesh's agricultural value chains. The Gates Foundation supports the initiative, developed under FINNOVISION, BRAC Bank's impact-driven SME Innovation Lab.
3 Imran joins IFIC Bank as AMD: Imran Ahmed has joined IFIC Bank PLC as Additional Managing Director. Prior to joining IFIC Bank, he served as Deputy Managing Director of National Bank PLC. His previous leadership positions were at Sonali Bank PLC, NRB Bank PLC and Bank Asia PLC.

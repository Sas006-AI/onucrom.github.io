A prolonged conflict could undermine remittance inflows, increase energy-import costs, fuel inflation and put further pressure on the external balances.
4 Bangladesh paid an additional USD 1.0 Bn in October to settle public-sector import payment obligations, particularly for fuel oil, gas and electricity, intensifying pressure on the country's foreign exchange market.
5 Concerns about Bangladesh's export competitiveness stoke up as the real effective exchange rate (REER) of the currency rose to 106.29 in September, indicating that the taka was overvalued by nearly 6.5%.
6 The government is planning to establish a joint-venture fertiliser factory in Saudi Arabia with an annual production capacity of 1.0 Mn tonnes of DAP. Bangladesh will guarantee the purchase of all fertiliser produced for 20 to 25 years, while Saudi Arabia will ensure an uninterrupted supply of gas and other required raw materials.
7 The government plans to scrap restriction that requires unused land of closed state-owned factories to be reused only for the same industry, opening 10,306 acres of land to any viable commercial or manufacturing enterprise.
8 Officials have said Bangladesh Railway has undertaken a BDT 37.79 Bn project to procure the coaches, four relief cranes and as many under-floor wheel lathe machines, aiming to ease a chronic shortage of passenger carriages.

5 Summit Power profit jumps 173%, recommends 10.50% cash dividend: Summit Power Limited (SPL) announced a 173% jump in consolidated net profit for FY26, reflecting a substantial improvement in its financial performance, and recommended a 10.50% cash dividend for shareholders.
6 Beximco Pharma recommends 60% cash dividend, posts BDT 9.0 Bn profit: Beximco Pharmaceuticals PLC recorded a net profit of BDT 9.0 Bn in FY26. The company recommending a 60% cash dividend, up from 47.5% in the previous fiscal year and its highest payout since at least 2020.
7 Marico’s Q2 profit flat as raw material costs bite: Sales revenue rose, but that did not help Marico Bangladesh secure earnings growth in the quarter to September this year, mainly due to pricier raw materials.

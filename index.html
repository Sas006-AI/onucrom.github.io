5 A shortage of lighter vessels has severely disrupted cargo unloading from mother vessels at the outer anchorage of Chattogram Port. Water Transport Coordination Cell has said increased imports of consumer goods had raised the number of arriving ships, while many lighter vessels remained stranded at river ports with cargo on board.
Sector & Industries
1 The ongoing unrest in industrial zones has inflicted severe damages on Bangladesh's RMG sector, with 317 surveyed factories suffering production losses worth USD 428 Mn and buyers shifting orders of USD 254 Mn to competitor destinations within 18 days.
2 Coal's share of total electricity generation rose from 6.24% in FY22 to 26.72% in FY25, marking a 328.21% increase over four years.
3 Bank deposits grew nearly 13% year-on-year in September, driven by strong remittance inflows and increased household savings and weak private investment. The growth accelerated from 10.74% in June, 11.1% in December 2025, 8% in June 2025 and 7.44% in December 2024.
4 Most banks are failing to comply with Bangladesh Bank's directive to cap the gap between lending and deposit interest rates at 4%, with the banking sector's average spread remaining close to 6% at the end of September.
5 The central bank has directed all scheduled banks to make effective use of the special exit facility to expedite the recovery of defaulted loans, according to a circular issued on November 20.
6 The government has made major changes to the Bank Company Act, 1991, to establish good governance in the banking sector, reduce family and political influence, strengthen defaulted loan recovery, and protect depositors’ interests, with the Ministry of Finance having finalised a draft of the amendments.
7 Loan case filing by defaulters is getting toughened, with a legal amendment that provides that stay-order petitions will cost 25% of the disputed money.
8 Any serving secretary or equivalent-grade official becomes disqualified to be appointed or nominated as chairman or director of banks wherein government holds shares or has sole ownership. No person will be appointed as a director of more than one bank or financial institution at the same time. Those who hold or have held within the past five years will be eligible for the post of chairman/director in that bank or financial institution.

2 Asiatic Lab’s Q1 earnings per share rise 12%: Asiatic Laboratories Limited reported a 12% year-on-year increase in EPS in Q1FY27.
3 Islami Bank clarifies no formal information on reported BDT 60 Bn state stake: Islami Bank Bangladesh PLC has denied having any formal information regarding a government plan to invest BDT 60 Bn in its equity, following a query from the Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE) over recent media reports.
4 Matin Spinning vice chairman to buy 1.024 Mn shares worth BDT 135 Mn: Mohammed Abdur Rahim, vice chairman of Matin Spinning Mills PLC, has expressed his intention to purchase 1.024 Mn shares of the company at prevailing market prices within the next 30 working days.

Stocks
1 Stocks rebound after 4-day slide as BSEC eases restrictions on frozen BO accounts: The stock market staged a broad-based recovery (4 November), with nearly 74% of traded securities advancing, as the Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC) decided to ease restrictions on selected beneficial owner (BO) accounts that had remained frozen for more than two years.
2 Cost cuts help listed power producers cling to growth path: Most listed private-sector power producers secured year-on-year profit growth in FY26, despite lower electricity generation compared with the previous year, plants remaining idle and expiry of power purchase agreements (PPAs).

Stocks
1 BSEC introduces single-window IPO review system: The Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC) has approved a single-window initial public offering (IPO) review system to make the application process more efficient, integrated and time-saving. Under the system, companies seeking to go public will submit their IPO proposals through the stock exchanges only.
2 BSEC gives brokers, merchant banks until 2030 to clear negative equity: The Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC) on Tuesday extended the deadline until 2030 for listed stock brokers and merchant banks to adjust their negative equity and unrealised losses, with a mandatory 25 % annual provision from 2027.
3 BSEC sets 30 November deadline for bonds to move from ATB to main board: The Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC) has decided to require issuers of bonds that were approved for listing on the Alternative Trading Board (ATB) but have yet to be listed there to apply for listing on the main boards of the country's stock exchanges by 30 November 2026.
4 43 struggling listed firms declare dividends, but sponsors and directors forgo payouts: As many as 43 struggling listed companies have recommended dividends exclusively for general shareholders for FY26, excluding their sponsors and directors from payouts, highlighting financial pressure on the firms and their efforts to retain their stock market listing categories.
5 Square Pharma Q1 profit rises to BDT 7.77 Bn: Square Pharmaceuticals PLC posted higher revenue and profit for Q1FY27, with net revenue rising 4% to BDT 22.4 Bn, up from BDT 21.6 Bn recorded in Q1FY26.
6 BB seeks explanation from bKash over 7,000 fake accounts: Bangladesh Bank has sought an explanation from bKash over the detection of more than 7,000 suspected fake accounts on its platform.
7 NRBC Bank to invest BDT 1.0 Bn in securities subsidiary: NRBC Bank will invest BDT 1 Bn in its subsidiary, NRBC Bank Securities Limited, within the next 30 days.

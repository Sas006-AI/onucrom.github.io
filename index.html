News Digest
As of : Wed Sep 30 2026 00:00:00 GMT+0600 (Bangladesh Standard Time)
Macro For the first time, Bangladesh Bank (BB) is set to announce its monetary policy on a quarterly basis today (Sep 30, 2026), replacing the existing six-month cycle. The central bank has decided to move to quarterly statements to make monetary policy more responsive to volatile domestic and global economic conditions.
Macro The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has found flaws in the proposed revenue-sector reforms tagged to the next credit programme for Bangladesh. IMF officials found that the revenue board gave importance on this fiscal year's higher revenue-generation plan. The proposal lacks required plans for the coming years as to how reforms will be carried out to boost revenue collection.
Macro Government borrowing from commercial banks rose more than fivefold in July-August of FY27, underscoring growing pressure on public finances and raising concerns about the availability of credit for the private sector.
Macro Bangladesh faces 66 operational and policy gaps in ensuring supply- chain traceability in major export sectors, which poses compliance problems in major export destinations. A comprehensive diagnostic presented by the Ministry of Commerce (MoC) at a recent closed-door workshop has revealed these gaps. The diagnostic was conducted by BUILD with the support of GIZ.
Macro Private sector credit growth has remained below 5% for six consecutive months, with growth standing at 4.75% at the end of August. Bankers and economists attributed the slowdown to weak business and investment demand, sluggish domestic consumption and economic activity, and an energy crisis that has increased operating costs.
Macro The Executive Committee of the National Economic Council on Tuesday approved 13 development projects involving BDT 157.13 Bn. The government will provide BDT 101.18 Bn, external sources BDT 52.12 Bn and the implementing agencies BDT 3.82 Bn.
Macro Concerns were raised at the latest meeting of the Executive Committee of the National Economic Council (Ecnec) after the costs of two metro rail projects financed by Japan nearly doubled. The government has decided to scrutinise Japanese loan conditions more carefully before accepting future financing.
Macro The Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS) is set to introduce an annual advance calendar showing when it will publish major statistics, making government data more predictable and accessible to users.
Macro Bangladesh has invited global oil and gas companies to invest in 26 offshore blocks, highlighting competitive terms under its Offshore Model Production Sharing Contract (PSC) 2026 and the Bay of Bengal's largely unexplored hydrocarbon potential at the World Energies Summit in London.
Macro Oil prices rose for a second ‌successive session on Tuesday as lingering concern over Middle East supply disruption brought about by the US-Israeli war on Iran outweighed signs of recovering crude exports.
Sector & Industries The securities regulator has allowed five listed banks to raise a combined BDT 35 Bn through bonds. The issuers are One Bank, Southeast Bank, Al-Arafah Islami Bank, Bank Asia, and BRAC Bank.
Sector & Industries Uncertainty over jobs is deepening among nearly 18,000 employees of Sammilito Islami Bank as their formal transfer to the new entity and a unified pay and human resources structure remain unresolved.
Sector & Industries After an 11-year gap, the government is set to provide Bangladesh Petroleum Corporation (BPC) with a BDT 45.0 Bn interest-free loan to help ensure uninterrupted fuel supplies. The loan will have to be repaid in half-yearly installments over five years, including a six-month grace period.
Sector & Industries Bangladesh Energy Regulatory Commission will conduct a fresh review of the financial statements of power-and energy-sector companies and has moved to enforce mandatory project approvals as part of efforts to strengthen regulatory oversight.
Sector & Industries State-run Bangladesh Petroleum Corporation (BPC) is planning to introduce artificial intelligence-based automation across the entire fuel import and marketing chain - from mother vessels at outer anchorage to consumers at petrol pumps - to curb pilferage, prevent contamination and reduce operating costs.
Sector & Industries The Asian Development Bank (ADB) would provide a USD 50 Mn loan to Bangladeshi private sector company Modern Syntex Limited (MSL) to manufacture modern, energy-efficient polyester chips.
Sector & Industries Bangladesh’s pharmaceutical industry may see some opportunities from the latest US tariff adjustment, but the immediate benefits are likely to be limited because the country does not currently manufacture many of the highly specialised medicines covered by the measure, industry leaders said.
Stocks The securities regulator has decided to allow Beximco Pharmaceuticals to overhaul its board with nine members proposed by the company, subject to the withdrawal of cases filed challenging the appointment of independent directors across the listed companies of the conglomerate. The board will include three sponsor-directors: Ahmed Sohail Fasihur Rahman, Osman Kaiser Chowdhury, and Iqbal Ahmed.
Stocks BRAC Bank has crossed USD 2.5 Bn in foreign remittance inflows during the first nine months of 2026, surpassing its previous full-year record of USD 2.2 Bn in 2025.
Stocks ACI has approved an additional equity investment of BDT 600 Mn in ACI CO-RO Bangladesh, its existing joint venture with Danish beverage maker CO-RO, to expand its presence in the beverage market.
Stocks A subsidiary of Southeast Bank PLC, TeleCash Limited received the final approval from Bangladesh Bank on September 24, to commence its commercial services.

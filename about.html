About Onucrom

Investment Discovery for Bangladesh

What We Do

Onucrom is a unified browsing space for investment opportunities in Bangladesh. We bring together public market instruments, government securities, and private fundraising opportunities in one place — with a clear, transparent Shariah-compliance lens that lets you filter according to your preferences.

We are not a broker. We are not a financial advisor. We are a discovery layer — a structured window into what exists, so you can decide what fits.

Why This Exists

Bangladesh's investment information is scattered. Stock data lives on one site. Government bond auctions on another. Private investment opportunities — the SME looking for growth capital, the startup raising its first round — have no centralized home at all. They circulate in closed networks, inaccessible to most.

For the investor who wants to browse, compare, and evaluate across these worlds, there is no single starting point. Onucrom is that starting point.

The Shariah Dimension

Bangladesh is majority Muslim. Many investors want clarity on whether an investment aligns with Islamic finance principles — but that clarity is hard to find. Screening methodologies vary, and most platforms ignore the question entirely.

We structure every listing with an explicit Shariah marker:

🟢 Compliant 🟡 Self-Certified ⚪ None

Compliant — Screened against published Shariah criteria and found to meet AAOIFI-aligned standards based on publicly available financials.

Self-Certified — The listing party has declared Shariah compliance; documentation is available for investor review.

None — No Shariah determination has been made or claimed.

This is a transparency framework, not a certification. We show you what is claimed and what is verifiable. The decision is yours.

How It Works

Public Markets: We aggregate listed securities with key financial metrics and Shariah tags. Use our on-page screening tools to filter by sector, valuation, dividend yield, and compliance status — all processing happens in your browser.

Government Securities: Browse treasury bills, bonds, and other government instruments with yield calculators and maturity ladders built directly into the page.

Private Fundraising: Investees self-submit their opportunities. Each listing shows the sector, amount sought, stage, location, and Shariah status. Investors browse, sort, and request introductions — Onucrom connects the parties. We do not handle funds or intermediate transactions.

Sanity Check: Our free investment evaluation tool lets you check any proposal against common fraud indicators, return reasonability, and structural red flags — before you commit a single taka.

Our Operating Principles

Transparency over authority. We label, we don't certify. Investors retain full responsibility for due diligence.

Capital efficiency. This platform is built on a near-zero-cost architecture. Utility should be proven before infrastructure is built.

Bangladesh first. Our data, our listings, our tools — built for the Bangladeshi investor, covering Bangladeshi opportunities.

No black boxes. Screening methodologies are published. Calculations happen client-side. Nothing is hidden.

Who Is Behind This

Onucrom is currently a solo-built, independently operated project. It exists because the gap exists. The platform will remain free for investors. Monetization, when introduced, will come from premium features and private-side listing services — not from selling data or restricting access.

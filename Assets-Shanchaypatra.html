📌 Data Transparency & Methodology
  • Rates are sourced from the Directorate of National Savings under the Ministry of Finance.
  • Returns are subject to a tiered structure: rates decrease for investments exceeding BDT 15 Lakh and BDT 30 Lakh.
  • Source tax (TDS) is automatically deducted from profits depending on TIN submission and aggregate investment amount.
📚 Investor Education: Shanchaypatra

Tiered Profit Rates

The government recently implemented a slab-based interest system. For example, your first 15 Lakh earns the highest interest bracket, 15-30 Lakh earns slightly less, and anything above 30 Lakh earns the lowest bracket rate.

Required Documents

  1. National ID (NID) copy.
  2. Passport size photographs (Applicant and Nominee).
  3. e-TIN Certificate (Mandatory for > BDT 2 Lakh).
  4. Bank Checkbook / Routing Number for EFT payout.

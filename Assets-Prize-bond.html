📌 Data Transparency & Methodology
  • Prize Bond results are officially drawn and published by Bangladesh Bank on behalf of the National Savings Directorate.
  • Prize amounts are subject to a 20% source tax at the time of claim.
  • The principal amount (100 BDT per bond) is guaranteed by the sovereign government and can be encashed at any time.
  • Onucrom is a listing and discovery platform; we do not trade, execute purchases, or hold investor funds directly.
📚 Investor Education: Prize Bonds

How It Works

The government holds a draw four times a year (31st Jan, 30th Apr, 31st Jul, 31st Oct). A single winning number in a tier wins the prize across all currently issued series (e.g., Ka, Kha, Ga...).

Claiming a Prize

  1. A bond must be held for at least two months before the draw date to be eligible.
  2. Winners must claim their prize within 2 years of the draw date.
  3. Submit the original bond and a copy of your NID to a designated Bank or Bangladesh Bank branch.

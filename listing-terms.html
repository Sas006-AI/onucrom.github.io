Listing Terms and Public-Link Policy

Applies to everything submitted through the Onucrom enlist and update forms.

1. What Onucrom is

Onucrom is an information and discovery platform. It is not a broker, dealer, investment adviser or Shariah certification body. It does not hold funds, intermediate investments or guarantee any outcome. A listing, a verification mark or a Shariah label is not a recommendation.

2. Who may enlist

Only a project sponsor, or an investor authorised to enlist the project, may submit an entry. You must be able to show your authority on request. One person (the same email address or the same mobile number) may hold up to 2 active listings.

3. What is public and what is private

Private (used only for verification, never displayed): your name, email, mobile number and social profile links. Public (displayed only after the listing is approved for display): project name, sector, offering type, stage, location (division), amount sought, stated return and basis, summary, your Shariah declaration, the public links you supply, and the verification mark we assign.

4. Your consent

By submitting, you consent to the public fields and every public link or proof link you supply being published on onucrom.com. Anything you link to becomes public through your listing. Do not enter anything you do not want published.

5. Link policy

  • Only https:// links to pages that are already public. Links to files that run or install (for example .exe, .bat, .msi, .apk, .js, .jar, scripts, macros) and archives (.zip, .rar, .7z) are rejected.
  • Shortened links, links containing credentials, and links to raw IP addresses are rejected.
  • Self-hosted proof is permitted only as a view-only Google Drive or Google Docs link, or a GitHub repository page link. Direct-download and release/archive links are rejected.
  • Links we publish open in a new tab and are marked as third-party. Onucrom does not control, endorse or accept liability for third-party websites or files. We may remove a link or a listing at any time if we judge a link unsafe.

6. What is not accepted

Claims of guaranteed, assured or risk-free returns; misleading, unverifiable or deliberately incomplete information; entries made without authority; and anything unlawful. Such entries are declined or disqualified, and we keep a record of declined entries.

7. Review, validity and renewal

Every entry is reviewed by a person. Review sittings are held daily at 12:00 and 18:00 (Dhaka time) and a decision can take a few hours or longer. We accept no more than 30 new entries per day. Enlistment approval and display approval each last 3 months unless renewed or withdrawn. We email reminders before expiry. To update or renew, use your Reference ID on the update page; updates are reviewed again before they replace what is shown. A listing that expires is hidden from the site.

8. Removal and records

You may ask us to remove your listing at any time by emailing onucromcorp@gmail.com with your Reference ID. Removal ends public display. We may keep private verification records and records of declined or disqualified entries as needed to prevent fraud and repeat abuse.

9. Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Onucrom accepts no liability for losses arising from any listing, link, verification mark, or dealing between listers and investors. Investors must do their own due diligence.

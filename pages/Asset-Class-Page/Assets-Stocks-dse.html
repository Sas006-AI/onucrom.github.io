📚 Investor Education: Equities

Dividends vs. Capital Gains

Dividends: Periodic cash or stock payouts distributed to shareholders from company profits.

Capital Gains: Profit earned when selling a stock at a higher price than the purchase price.

How to Trade in Bangladesh

  1. Select a registered stockbroker (Brokerage House).
  2. Open a Beneficiary Owner (BO) Account via CDBL.
  3. Fund your account and trade via the broker's DSE Mobile App or trading portal.

Shariah Alternative (DSES)

Investors seeking halal equities can track the DSES (DSE Shariah Index), which filters out companies dealing in conventional banking, alcohol, tobacco, and highly leveraged debt.

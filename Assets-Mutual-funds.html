📌 Data Transparency & Methodology
  • NAV (Net Asset Value) figures are declared weekly by Asset Management Companies (AMCs).
  • Closed-end funds are traded on the DSE and often trade at a premium or discount to their actual NAV.
  • Onucrom is a listing and discovery platform; we do not trade, execute purchases, or hold investor funds directly.
📚 Investor Education: Mutual Funds

Open-End vs. Closed-End

Open-End: Bought and sold directly through the AMC at the current NAV. No maturity date.

Closed-End: Fixed number of shares, traded on the stock exchange like regular stocks. Subject to market supply/demand.

Understanding NAV

Net Asset Value (NAV) represents a fund's per-share market value. It is calculated by dividing the total value of all the cash and securities in a fund's portfolio, minus any liabilities, by the number of outstanding shares.
